# Liftly — Project Goal

## What Liftly is

Liftly is a workout app for gym beginners. Users log their sets, reps, weights and effort. A deterministic progression engine decides what to lift next. Claude explains those decisions in plain language, using the user's own logged training.

Liftly is a web app, installable on the phone home screen (PWA). Units are kilograms (kg).

## Target user

- Beginners with 0–12 months of consistent training.
- Goal: building muscle (hypertrophy).
- Training in a standard commercial gym, in India.
- Following a 3-day or 4-day program assigned by Liftly.
- Currently tracking in a notes app, or not tracking at all.

## Core problem

Beginners often don't know what to lift next. They either repeat the same weights for months, progress too fast and get hurt, or stop because they can't see progress. Note-taking apps record workouts but never say what to do next.

## Main product promise

> Liftly tells you exactly what to lift next session, and why, based on your own training history.

## What Liftly is not

- Not a human coach. It doesn't replace one.
- Not a general chatbot. There is no free-form chat in V1.
- Not medical advice. Liftly never diagnoses pain or injury.

## MVP success definition

The MVP is successful if a closed beta of 3–5 beginners (Phase 0, then the wider beta) shows:

1. **Retention:** testers are still training in week 4.
2. **Trust in targets:** testers accept the suggested targets without editing them most of the time.
3. **Debrief value:** testers find the post-workout debriefs useful.

[ASSUMPTION] The numeric thresholds used to judge these (from Phase 0 planning, not yet validated):
- Target acceptance ≥ 80% of exposures.
- Debriefs sent unedited ≥ 80% of the time, average tester rating ≥ 4/5, zero factual errors.
- Complete logging for ≥ 90% of sessions.

Final thresholds are set after Phase 0 results are reviewed.
